Privacy Policy

MYRIAD LEGENDS (萬象傳奇錄) · https://mylegends.org · Last updated 2026-11-22

MYRIAD LEGENDS is a free, non-commercial educational card game. There are no ads, no tracking scripts, and we never sell or share personal data.

What is stored in your browser

Your card collection, settings and progress are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can export or import a backup file at any time from the Collection page.

Cloud save (optional)

If you create a transfer code or sign in with Google, a copy of your game save (cards, progress, settings) is stored on our server (Cloudflare D1) so you can play on several devices. The save is linked to a random transfer code. Anyone who knows your code can load that save, so keep it private.

Player profile (nickname and avatar)

The Player Profile lets you choose a nickname and an avatar. Your nickname is a name you type yourself; it is not your Google name. The avatar is either one of six system presets or a cropped circular portrait of an EXR character card you have pulled. Your nickname, avatar and frame choice are stored in this browser's local storage and, if you use cloud save, in your cloud save file too. They travel with your save and are never used to identify you to Google or any third party. We do not maintain a public directory of nicknames.

Google sign-in (optional)

When you sign in with Google, we only keep Google's anonymous account identifier (the "sub" ID) to find your save. We do not store your Google name, email address, photo or any other Google account data, and we request no extra permissions. This is separate from the nickname and avatar you set in the Player Profile, which are your own choices and are saved with your game progress.

Global pack counter

When you open packs, the site sends only a number (how many packs were opened) to a shared counter. No identity is attached.

Third parties

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address) for security. Some card pictures are loaded directly from Wikipedia / Wikimedia Commons, so those servers receive normal web requests from your browser. Google sign-in is provided by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser data removes the local save. To delete a cloud save, open an issue at github.com/seanown/myriad-legends/issues with your transfer code (shown on the Collection page) and we will remove it.

Children

The game does not ask for any personal information and is suitable for all ages.

隱私權政策

萬象傳奇錄是免費、非商業的教育卡牌遊戲。沒有廣告、沒有追蹤程式，也絕不販售或分享個人資料。

存在你瀏覽器裡的資料

卡牌收藏、設定與進度存在你自己裝置的瀏覽器本機儲存中。你可以隨時在「圖鑑」頁匯出或匯入備份檔。

雲端存檔（選用）

如果你建立引繼碼或用 Google 登入，你的遊戲存檔（卡牌、進度、設定）會在我們的伺服器（Cloudflare D1）存一份，方便在多個裝置遊玩。存檔對應一組隨機引繼碼，知道這組碼的人就能讀取存檔，請妥善保管。

玩家檔案（暱稱與頭像）

玩家檔案可讓你設定暱稱與頭像。暱稱是你自行輸入的名稱，不是你的 Google 名稱。頭像為六個系統預設頭像之一，或由你已抽到的 EXR 人物卡卡圖裁剪成的圓形人頭。你的暱稱、頭像與頭像框會儲存在本機瀏覽器；若使用雲端存檔，也會一併存入你的雲端存檔。這些資料隨存檔移動，不會用來向 Google 或任何第三方識別你的身分，我們也不建立公開的暱稱名錄。

Google 登入（選用）

用 Google 登入時，我們只保存 Google 提供的匿名帳號編號（sub ID）來找到你的存檔。我們不保存你的 Google 姓名、電子郵件、頭像或任何其他 Google 帳號資料，也不要求額外權限。這與你在玩家檔案中自行設定的暱稱與頭像不同，後者是你自己的選擇，並隨遊戲進度一併保存。

全服開包計數

開包時，網站只會送出一個數字（開了幾包）到共用計數器，不附帶任何身分資料。

第三方

網站架設在 Cloudflare，Cloudflare 可能為了安全保留一般技術紀錄（例如 IP 位址）。部分卡牌圖片直接從維基百科／維基共享資源載入，這些伺服器會收到你瀏覽器的一般網頁請求。Google 登入由 Google 提供，適用 Google 自己的隱私權政策。

刪除資料

清除瀏覽器資料即可刪除本機存檔。要刪除雲端存檔，請到 github.com/seanown/myriad-legends/issues 留下你的引繼碼（顯示在圖鑑頁），我們會將它移除。
